EU AI Act risk levels

The EU AI Act sorts AI systems into four risk levels. Click a level to see what it covers and what it requires.

TIER · HIGH RISK — ANNEX III

Allowed, with strict requirements.

CV screening, credit scoring, exam proctoring, critical infrastructure. Allowed, but only with a full set of controls in place.
